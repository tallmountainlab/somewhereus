--- title: Privacy policy description: How somewhereus.com handles your information. ---

Last updated October 31, 2026.

What we collect

We do not require accounts. If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored by our email provider and used only to send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views. These do not identify you personally.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third-party vendors, including Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the partner site may set a cookie to record that you came from somewhereus.com. We also use a script from Travelpayouts, an affiliate network, which can turn links to travel partners into affiliate links and may set its own cookies. See our affiliate disclosure.
