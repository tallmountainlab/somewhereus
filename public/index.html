Then and now

Same roads, same views, a different kind of camera

Long before phone cameras, a trip to Bryce Canyon or Zion was something you recorded on film, like the photographer in this early National Park Service picture by George A. Grant. Families drove two-lane highways, stopped at motor courts and came home with a box of slides.

Today you book some parks online and the roads are smoother, but the view from the rim is the same one that filled those old slide shows. That is the trip these guides are here to help you take.
