About somewhereus.com

Remember the family road trips: the station wagon, the cooler, the motel with a pool and the souvenir shop with the rubber tomahawks and the postcards? somewhereus.com is about getting that feeling back. We are based in Utah, surrounded by some of the most beautiful parks in the country, so that is where our guides start.

From there, we cover road trips, national parks and weekend getaways all across America, with a soft spot for the classic diners, old lodges and historic highways that make a trip feel like a memory before it is even over.

How we write our guides

Every guide mixes a little history and a lot of practical advice.

  • We focus on the decisions that make or break a trip: routes, drive times, permits, reservations and the right season.
  • Every guide shows the date it was last reviewed, and we revisit guides regularly.
  • Park rules and road conditions change, so we link to official sources and ask you to check before you go.

Share your memories

Do you have an old photo, a postcard or a story from one of the places we write about? We would love to hear it, and with your permission we may share it in a guide. Send it to hello@somewhereus.com.
